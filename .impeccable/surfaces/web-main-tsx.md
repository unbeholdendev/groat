## Direction contract

THESIS: One number, stated loud; one line, at thumb reach. Each screen opens on the ground with the month's spend at 60px in ink, like a banknote's denomination; under it the month stands as a skyline of days. A frosted dock takes "ramen 24.50 #friends" or "?" questions. It refuses both the grey ledger it replaces and the finance default of a FAB, a form and a donut.

OWN-WORLD: Cool ground (#F5F7FB / #0D0F15) carrying every headline figure in Geist ink; no coloured field. Cobalt (#3250F0 light, #779BFF dark) only for what is live or commits, and this period's spend line. Level 1 panels with inset hairlines, Level 2 frosted glass for the dock and chips. Eight matched OKLCH category hues, clear of cobalt, as tinted emoji tiles, Day skyline segments and share tracks. Geist Mono for every column figure. Dark is the hero theme.

STORY: Open the app: the month's total counts up, a plain mono delta says how it compares with last month by today, and the Day skyline lifts, one stacked column per day; tap a day to jump to its rows. Type one line, Enter, the new row grows in and a receipt offers Undo. Stats draws this month's cumulative cobalt line over last month's ghost, with today marked, then category share tracks.

FIRST VIEWPORT: On the ground: borderless chevrons either side of "October" (15px, Ink 2), filter far right; total at 60px; delta with "vs Sept by today"; the 112px Day skyline with 1 and 31 under it and a top-three legend. Then day groups as panels of 62px rows (tile, note over category and tags, mono amount). Frosted dock at the bottom: 48px bar over four tabs, one surface.

FORM: Bold consumer fintech, pinned by the user (no roll, no comps): "The Denomination", code-led. Signature: the Day skyline, and the dock. Signature interaction: the hero numeral counts up, the skyline lifts and the spend line draws itself; rows grow in on add.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
